# Provenance

This file records where the construction came from and what carrying it
forward asks of you. It is not a licence. The licence is in `LICENSE` and
governs the code; this governs nothing, and is here because a technical
record that hides its own origin is worth less than one that states it.

## Where it came from

The five parts in this repository were not designed from the agent
literature. They were taken out of a longer body of practical work, written
in Russian over 2026, concerned with how one party can be held to an
account of a change in the world when the only witness is the party making
the claim. That body of work is not published here and is not needed to
use this. If anything in `SPEC.md` depends on reading it, that is a defect
in `SPEC.md`.

What was carried over is one idea with a narrow scope: a promise names its
own means of being checked before any action is taken, and the sentence
"it is done" is held until that means answers. The parts that could not be
stated as a record, a state or a refusal were left behind.

## What is owed

The construction was arrived at in exchange with another party — a language
model, over long sessions — and neither half of it is separable now. The
wording of the five records, the four terminal states and the rule that one
receipt attests only what its own source observes came out of that
exchange. Saying so costs nothing and is simply accurate.

Earlier formulations are kept rather than tidied away. Where a later
version of a rule disagrees with an earlier one, both stay readable at
their own version numbers, and the later one does not acquire the right to
edit the earlier after the fact. `SPEC.md` §7 states this for the
specification; it is the general habit of the work this came from.

## What is asked

Nothing enforceable. Three things, as a matter of practice:

1. **Report what broke, not only what worked.** A case where the adapter
   collapsed back into trusting the agent is worth more here than a
   successful deployment.
2. **If you reimplement it, give your version its own number** and keep this
   one readable. A reimplementation that silently claims to be version 0.1
   makes two different things answer to one name.
3. **If you extend it, say what you added.** A new terminal state, a new
   escrowed right or a new class of adapter changes what the construction
   can be held to, and the next reader needs to know which parts are
   0.1 and which are yours.
